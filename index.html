Why ECO-CYCLE-DN?

Recycling should be simple and accessible for everyone.

The Problem

  • Improper waste segregation
    People often mix recyclable materials with regular waste.
  • Difficulty finding recycling centers
    It can be hard to find nearby places to recycle.
  • Lack of recycling information
    Some people do not know what materials can be recycled.

Our Solution

ECO-CYCLE-DN provides one digital platform that connects people with recycling centers, gives waste segregation information, allows pickup scheduling, and rewards users for recycling.

About Us

Built in Davao del Norte, for Davao del Norte.

Our Startup

ECO-CYCLE-DN is a digital recycling platform that helps individuals, households, students, and businesses manage recyclable waste. It provides a center locator, pickup scheduling, segregation guides, and a rewards system.

How We Sustain the Platform

Pickup fees, business subscriptions, partner commissions, sponsored campaigns, and advertising.

Our Mission

To make recycling simple, accessible, and rewarding for people in Davao del Norte.

Our Vision

A cleaner Davao del Norte where proper waste segregation and recycling are part of everyday life.

Our Objectives

  • Help residents find nearby recycling centers.
  • Make recyclable waste pickup easy to schedule.
  • Teach proper waste segregation.
  • Encourage recycling through points and rewards.
  • Partner with junk shops, barangays, schools, and businesses.

Who We Serve

Households

Families who want an easy way to recycle materials.

Students

Learners who practice proper waste segregation.

Communities

Schools, barangays, and environmental groups.

Businesses

Offices and stores that need convenient waste pickup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ECO-CYCLE-DN?

A digital recycling platform for Davao del Norte.

What can I recycle?

Paper, plastic, metal, and glass. Check the Segregation Guide for more information.

How do I schedule a pickup?

Choose your recyclable items, date, time, and location, then confirm your request.

How do I earn points?

You receive points based on the materials collected after each pickup.

Who can use the platform?

Households, students, businesses, and community organizations in Davao del Norte.

Is the app available now?

ECO-CYCLE-DN is currently a prototype tested with 16 potential users.
